# Zhixing Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Zhixing welcomes respectful collaboration in English or Chinese. Discuss ideas and code on their merits, give actionable feedback, make space for different experience levels, and assume good faith while still addressing concrete risks.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory or sexualized language, sustained disruption, impersonation, deliberate publication of another person's private information, and retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This standard applies to project-controlled GitHub issues, pull requests, reviews, and other repository interactions. It also applies when someone is representing the project in a public project-related space.

## Reporting and enforcement

When a concern is visible in public repository content and can be reported without exposing private information, open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/Tandem-Agents/zhixing/issues) with a link to the content and a concise description. Do not publish private messages, personal data, credentials, or other sensitive material to support a report.

The project maintainers are responsible for reviewing conduct reports and moderating project-controlled spaces. They may remove harmful content, close or limit disruptive threads, warn participants, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community. They should handle reports consistently, avoid unnecessary disclosure, and recuse themselves from decisions involving their own conduct.

Behavior or content that may violate GitHub's Community Guidelines or Terms of Service can also be reported through GitHub's platform tools described in [Reporting abuse or spam](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam). Available reporting controls and destinations depend on the content, repository settings, and the reporter's relationship to the repository. A report to GitHub addresses potential platform violations; it does not replace project-maintainer review of project conduct.

No private project conduct-reporting contact is currently published. If a concern cannot be reported safely without private information, do not disclose it publicly; use GitHub's reporting path when it concerns a platform violation, or wait until the repository publishes a real private project contact. Security vulnerabilities are a separate matter and must not be posted as conduct complaints or public issues.
